# Call Me Crazy: slogans and brand lines (working list)

Rules: clean language on the website, no swearing. "Crazy" means personality and attitude, never mental health. Aim the attitude at norms and sameness, never at women or bodies.

- **Own Your Crazy.** (hero headline)
